## Final decision
PROCEED WITH CAUTION for a small pilot; heuristic early-screen score 5/10.
The proposed additive contribution is equal-verification-cost improvement in unseen-area
building dwelling-unit assignment, using train-only parcel counts and independently verified
per-building unit anchors while requiring only imagery/georeference at inference.
Known aggregate learning, population disaggregation, dual views, role heads and uncertainty
propagation do not alone establish novelty. Current data do not prove label attribution or
target feature scale causes the eleven persistent LA misses.

Immediate priority: matched current-CNN crop experiment, then only justified structural work.
Required simple competitors: clean direct building-unit supervision, ambiguous-label masking,
naive label copying, plain sum-loss supervision, and direct image-to-exposure regression.
Falsification: aggregate sums improve but building allocation does not, or anchored method
fails to exceed direct supervised units at equal verification cost.

No model training, weight/label edits, production replacement, manuscript edits or commit/push.
